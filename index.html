Overview

Rollin helps teachers take attendance digitally — creating registers, marking students present, absent, or on leave, and generating reports. It's built to work fully offline: the app doesn't need an internet connection to function, and it doesn't require you to create an account.

This policy tells you what information the app collects, how it's used, and who can see it. If a section doesn't apply to you (for example, if you never enable crash reporting-related permissions), it still explains what would happen if it did.

Data we collect

There are two, clearly separate categories of data connected to Rollin. We've kept them apart deliberately, because they're handled very differently.

Data you enter

Stays on your device. Never sent anywhere.

  • Register and class names
  • Student names
  • Daily attendance records (present / absent / leave)
  • Reports and PDFs you generate

Crash & diagnostic data

Sent to Firebase Crashlytics automatically.

  • Crash logs and error traces
  • Device model and OS version
  • App version
  • Approximate, coarse device identifiers used to group crash reports

Crash reports never include the registers, student names, or attendance data you've entered — they contain only technical information about the app's behavior, so we can find and fix bugs.

How we use it

Data you enter

This data is used only to run the app on your device — displaying your registers, calculating attendance percentages, and generating the PDF reports you ask for. We never see it, because it never leaves your phone or tablet.

Crash & diagnostic data

Used only to identify bugs, crashes, and stability issues, so we can fix them in future updates. We don't use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond improving the app's reliability.

Storage & security

All registers, student names, and attendance records are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. There's no cloud sync, no backend server, no remote database, and no automatic backup of this data — Rollin simply doesn't have anywhere else for it to go.

Because there's no account or login system, there's no password or credential of yours for us to store or lose. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device settings, permanently removes this data.

Sharing & third parties

We don't sell, rent, or share the data you enter into Rollin with anyone — we can't, since it never reaches us in the first place.

The one third-party service Rollin uses is Firebase Crashlytics, operated by Google, which processes the crash and diagnostic data described above. You can read Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle this data on our behalf.

Rollin contains no advertising and no other third-party analytics tools.

A note on student data

As a teacher using Rollin, the student names and attendance records you enter are held entirely on your own device, under your control. Rollin (the app and its developer) never collects, receives, or has access to this information.

Because you are the one entering and storing this information, you're responsible for using it in line with your school's data protection policies and any laws that apply to student records in your region — for example, keeping your device secured with a lock screen, and deleting registers you no longer need.

If you export a PDF report and share it with a parent, administrator, or colleague, that sharing happens through your device's own share tools (email, messaging apps, etc.) — Rollin doesn't transmit or store a copy of anything you export.

Your choices

  • Delete your data anytime — clear the app's storage or uninstall Rollin from your device settings.
  • No account to manage — there's nothing to close or deactivate, since Rollin never asked you to create one.
  • Questions about crash data — email us at support@letsrollin.com and we're happy to explain exactly what's collected.

Children's privacy

Rollin is designed for use by teachers and educators, not by children directly. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children, and any student names entered are provided by the teacher, stored only on the teacher's own device, and never transmitted to us, as explained above.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy — for example, if a future version of Rollin adds a new feature that changes what data is collected — we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. We'd encourage checking back occasionally, especially after updating the app.

In particular, if we introduce an optional account and cloud-sync feature in the future (so your registers can be recovered across devices without relying on your device's own backup), we'll revise this policy before that feature ships to clearly explain what's collected, how it's stored on our servers, and what choices you have.
